About This Database

Suffragettes on British Screens is a research database cataloguing 138 fiction films on suffragette and women's suffrage themes released in Britain between 1906 and 1918. It is compiled from systematic archival research in the two principal British film trade papers of the period — the Kinematograph Weekly (KW) and The Bioscope (BS) — and aims to provide a reliable, source-documented record of how the suffrage movement was represented on the early British screen.

Many of these films are lost; for a large number of them, the trade-paper notices, synopses and advertisements gathered here are the fullest surviving record of their content.

Sources and Methodology

Primary sources. The database is built on a systematic search of the Kinematograph Weekly (1900–1918) and The Bioscope (1908–1918), accessed through the British Newspaper Archive (BNA), using the search terms 'suffrage' and 'suffragette'. Entries identified in scholarly literature were added and traced back to the trade papers wherever possible.

Synopses. Plot synopses are drawn exclusively from the primary sources. Where no contemporary description has been located, the field is left blank rather than reconstructed from secondary sources.

Titles. Films are listed under the titles used in KW/BS at the time of release. Variant titles — including those used by modern databases — are recorded in the Alternative Titles field. Original-language titles of non-English-language films are likewise recorded as alternative titles.

Years. The year given is the year of the film's UK release, as stated in KW/BS release notices ('Released …'), which may fall in the year after the film was first reviewed or advertised. Where no release date is given, the year of the trade-paper notices is used.

Production companies. Variant forms of the same company's name (e.g. 'Pathé', 'Pathé Frères', 'Pathé Films') are standardised to a single form. Successor or distinct companies are kept separate (e.g. Cricks & Sharp, Cricks & Martin and Martin Films; Éclair and Éclair American).

Film lengths. Footage figures are taken from KW/BS notices. Where the two papers disagree, the KW figure is adopted and the discrepancy is recorded in the Notes.

Verification status. Every entry carries one of three labels:

  • Verified — corroborated in KW and/or BS;
  • Discrepancy found — the sources disagree on a point of fact; the primary source given priority is stated in the Notes;
  • Unverified — no KW/BS corroboration has yet been located.

Supplementary sources. IMDb, the BFI and AFI catalogues, and scholarly publications are used for cross-reference and supplementary information (such as US release data and production credits). Wherever such material is used, the source is identified in the Notes.

A Note on Links

Links to Kinematograph Weekly and The Bioscope pages point to the British Newspaper Archive; a subscription may be required to view full pages. The Bioscope and other trade papers of the period can also be consulted free of charge through the Media History Digital Library.

Version

Version 17, last revised 1 October 2026. Archived on Zenodo under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21321666, which covers all versions and always resolves to the latest one. The database is a work in progress and is revised as further primary evidence is located. Substantive changes between versions are documented in the revision notes.
