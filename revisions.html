Version 16 12 July 2026 — current

A systematic collation of all 138 entries against the compiler's Kinematograph Weekly / The Bioscope research files (87 cells revised).

  • Footage added for 30 films from KW/BS notices, each with its source date (footage now recorded for 126 of 138 entries) — including The Tragedy of the Vote (No. 74, 835 ft), The Pickpocket (No. 79, 1,013 ft), The Suffragette (No. 99, Britannia, 1,875 ft), Stemmeretskvinden (No. 109, 2,345 ft), When the Men Left Town (No. 117, 2,096 ft) and A Woman's Awakening (No. 138, 4,500 ft).
  • Seven source discrepancies resolved: Suffrage and the Man (No. 70) corrected from 1,000 ft to 552 ft (KW 24/07/1913); Nabbem Joins the Force (No. 81) 415 ft (KW 30/10/1913); A Sham Suffragette (No. 86) 1,000 ft (BS 04/09/1913); A Cow-Town's Reformation (No. 89) 1,036 ft (KW), BS figure of 1,035 ft noted; Die Suffragette (No. 91) 3,500 ft; Mrs. Manly's Baby (No. 104) 996 ft (KW), BS figure of 995 ft noted; Polidor e le suffragette (No. 108) 570 ft.
  • References added: BS 10/04/1913 for The Suffragette (No. 55, Selig); BS 17/06/1909 for Votes for Women: A Caricature (No. 14); BS 11/12/1913 for A Cow-Town's Reformation (No. 89); further KW/BS references recorded for Nos. 1, 74, 119 and 136.
  • Title spelling note added for The Suffragette's Battle in Nuttyville (No. 123): KW 12/11/1914 prints 'The Suffragette Battle of Nuttyville'.
  • Website regenerated from Version 16, with contemporary images for 16 films and this About/Methodology apparatus.

Version 15 11 July 2026

  • Working-process annotations ('Previously Unverified — now confirmed…') removed from the Notes of 16 entries; all source citations retained.
  • Coon Town Suffragettes (No. 103): synopsis replaced with the full KW 09/04/1914 description and footage (471 ft) added. A period racial epithet in the original text is rendered neutrally in the synopsis; the film's title is retained as a historical record.
  • Pataclet Loves a Suffragette (No. 82): verification status changed to 'Discrepancy found', reflecting the KW (730 ft) / BS (780 ft) footage disagreement recorded in the Notes.
  • Link labelling corrected: all KW and BS links point to the British Newspaper Archive.

Version 14 11 July 2026

  • Pataclet Loves a Suffragette (No. 82) corrected against KW 20/11/1913: production company Cosmograph Films (previously misattributed to Pathé Frères), footage 730 ft, synopsis replaced with the KW-based text. Cosmograph attribution corroborated by 1895: Revue d'histoire du cinéma.
  • The Rival Candidates (No. 25): Edison attribution re-grounded in BS 27/04/1911 ('Released May 3rd. Length 1,000 ft.').

Version 13 5 July 2026

  • Baseline of the current numbering: entries re-ordered by UK release year and renumbered 1–138; some ninety verification checks applied; final three-pass structural validation.
